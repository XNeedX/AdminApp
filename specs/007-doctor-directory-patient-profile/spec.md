## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Create a doctor profile (Priority: P1)

A signed-in Receptionist on the "Doctors" page opens the "Create doctor" form, enters the
doctor's personal and professional details, optionally attaches a photo, and confirms.
The system creates the doctor's account and profile, generates the doctor's password, and
emails the credentials to the doctor. The Receptionist can abandon the form at any time
through a confirmation dialog.

**Why this priority**: Onboarding doctors is the entry point of the whole doctor
lifecycle. Without a created profile there is nothing to list, edit, filter, or assign to
appointments.

**Independent Test**: Sign in as a Receptionist, open "Doctors", click "Create doctor",
fill every required field with valid values, and confirm. Verify that the new doctor
appears in the directory and receives the credentials email. Then repeat and cancel the
form through the dialog to verify that nothing is saved.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Receptionist is on the "Doctors" page, **When** they click "Create doctor",
   **Then** the creation form is displayed with photo, first name, last name, middle name,
   date of birth, email, specialization, office, career start year and status, plus
   "Confirm" and "Cancel" actions.
2. **Given** the creation form is open, **When** any required field is empty or invalid,
   **Then** "Confirm" remains disabled.
3. **Given** all required fields are valid and the date fields are not in the future,
   **When** the Receptionist clicks "Confirm", **Then** the doctor profile is stored, the
   doctor account is provisioned with a system-generated password, the credentials email
   is sent to the doctor's email address, the form closes and the directory shows the new
   doctor.
4. **Given** the Receptionist attached an image and entered a middle name, **When** the
   form is confirmed with otherwise valid data, **Then** the photo and middle name are
   stored with the profile.
5. **Given** the creation form contains entered or selected data, **When** the Receptionist
   clicks "Cancel", **Then** the dialog "Do you really want to cancel? Entered data will
   not be saved." is shown. Choosing "Yes" closes the form without saving and returns to
   the "Doctors" page; choosing "No" closes the dialog and keeps the form data intact.
6. **Given** the entered email already belongs to an existing user, **When** the field
   loses focus or the form is submitted, **Then** the message "User with this email
   already exists" is shown and no second account is created.
7. **Given** the photo upload fails, **When** the Receptionist confirms, **Then** no
   partial doctor profile is created and the failure is reported so the form can be
   retried.

### User Story 2 - Doctors view a patient profile (Priority: P4)

A signed-in Doctor views "My schedule". For an approved appointment the patient's name is
an active link that opens the patient's profile; for a not-approved appointment the link
is disabled. The profile shows personal information by default and appointment results on
a second tab.

**Why this priority**: Doctors need patient context during a visit, but this journey
depends on the doctor schedule and on the doctor directory/profile foundations, so it is
delivered last.

**Independent Test**: Seed a doctor's schedule with one approved and one not-approved
appointment. Verify that only the approved appointment exposes a working patient link,
that the profile opens on "Personal information", and that switching to "Appointment
results" shows the patient's recorded results or an empty state.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Doctor's schedule contains an appointment whose status is "Approved",
   **When** the Doctor clicks the patient's name, **Then** the patient profile opens.
2. **Given** a Doctor's schedule contains an appointment whose status is not "Approved",
   **When** the Doctor views that row, **Then** the patient's name is displayed as disabled
   and cannot be opened.
3. **Given** the patient profile is open, **Then** it has two tabs, "Personal information"
   and "Appointment results", with "Personal information" selected by default.
4. **Given** the "Personal information" tab is selected, **Then** it shows the patient's
   photo, first name, last name, middle name (when present), phone number and date of
   birth.
5. **Given** the "Appointment results" tab is selected, **Then** it lists the patient's
   appointments with recorded results, most recent first, each showing the appointment
   date, doctor, service and the recorded complaints, conclusion and recommendations; an
   empty state is shown when the patient has no recorded results.
6. **Given** the patient profile cannot be loaded, **Then** an explanatory error state is
   shown instead of an empty or partial profile.
7. **Given** a user without access to the patient profile (for example, a Receptionist or
   a Doctor without an approved appointment for that patient) opens the profile address
   directly, **Then** the profile content is not shown.

### User Story 3 - Edit a doctor profile (Priority: P3)

A Doctor can open their own profile and a Receptionist can open any doctor's profile from
the directory. Clicking "Edit" makes the profile fields editable. "Save changes" persists
valid edits and returns to view mode; "Cancel" asks for confirmation before discarding
them.

**Why this priority**: Corrections are required for accurate doctor data, but the
directory and creation flows already deliver standalone value.

**Independent Test**: Open a doctor profile as a Receptionist, click "Edit", verify that
"Save changes" stays disabled while a required field is invalid, save a valid change and
verify view mode shows the new value. Repeat and cancel through the dialog, confirming
that "Yes" discards and "No" preserves the edits.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a doctor profile is displayed in view mode, **When** the user clicks "Edit",
   **Then** the editable fields become inputs and the actions "Save changes" and "Cancel"
   are displayed.
2. **Given** the profile is in edit mode, **When** any required field is empty or invalid,
   **Then** "Save changes" remains disabled.
3. **Given** all required fields are valid, **When** the user clicks "Save changes",
   **Then** the changes are stored, view mode is shown again and displays the updated
   values.
4. **Given** the profile is in edit mode with changes, **When** the user clicks "Cancel",
   **Then** the dialog "Do you really want to cancel? Changes will not be saved." is
   shown. Choosing "Yes" returns to view mode without saving; choosing "No" closes the
   dialog and keeps edit mode with the entered data.
5. **Given** the profile is in edit mode, **When** a date field would be set after the
   current date, **Then** the value is rejected and "Save changes" remains disabled.
6. **Given** a Doctor opened their own profile, **When** they edit and save, **Then** the
   change is visible on the same profile. **Given** a Receptionist opened a doctor from the
   directory, the same save updates that doctor's profile and the directory.

### User Story 4 - Browse, filter and change doctor status (Priority: P2)

A signed-in Receptionist opens the "Doctors" page and sees a table of all doctors. The
table shows each doctor's full name, specialization, status, date of birth and office.
Status can be changed directly in the table and is saved immediately. The list can be
narrowed by doctor name, specialization and office, and the filters can be combined.

**Why this priority**: The directory is the operational hub for receptionists and the
entry point for creation and editing. It is independently valuable even before creation
and editing are complete.

**Independent Test**: Sign in as a Receptionist, open "Doctors", verify the columns and the
status control, change one status and reload to confirm persistence, then combine the name,
specialization and office filters and verify that only matching doctors remain.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Receptionist opens the "Doctors" page, **Then** a table of doctors is shown
   with columns: full name, specialization, status, date of birth and office, plus a
   "Create doctor" button.
2. **Given** the table shows a doctor, **When** the Receptionist selects another value in
   that doctor's status dropdown, **Then** the new status is saved immediately without a
   separate save action, and the row reflects the new status.
3. **Given** a status change fails to save, **Then** the dropdown returns to the previous
   status and an error is reported.
4. **Given** the Receptionist selects a doctor in the "Search by name" combobox, **Then**
   the table shows only that doctor; clearing the selection restores the other filters'
   results.
5. **Given** an office is selected in the office filter, **When** the Receptionist opens
   the specialization filter, **Then** it offers only specializations of doctors working
   in that office.
6. **Given** a specialization is selected in the specialization filter, **When** the
   Receptionist opens the office filter, **Then** it offers only offices where doctors
   with that specialization work.
7. **Given** more than one filter is set, **Then** the table shows only doctors matching
   all selected filters, and clearing all filters restores the complete directory.
8. **Given** no doctor matches the current filters, **Then** an empty state explains that
   no doctors match and the filters can be changed.
9. **Given** the Receptionist clicks a doctor's full name in the table, **Then** that
   doctor's profile opens.
10. **Given** the directory is loading or fails to load, **Then** a loading indicator or a
    retryable error state is shown instead of an empty table.

### Edge Cases

- The email becomes taken between validation and submission: the save is rejected with
  "User with this email already exists" and the form remains open with the data intact.
- A photo is selected but upload fails: the doctor is not created and the Receptionist can
  retry; no profile without the intended photo is silently created.
- The form is submitted twice quickly or "Confirm"/"Save changes" is activated again while
  saving: only one profile is created and only one update is applied.
- A required field contains only whitespace: it is treated as empty.
- A date of birth or career start year in the future, or an invalid calendar date, is
  rejected.
- A doctor is deleted or deactivated elsewhere while the directory is open: refreshing the
  list reflects the change; a status change on a missing doctor reports an error and
  restores the previous value.
- A selected office or specialization disappears from the available options: the filter is
  reset to "all" instead of leaving the table permanently empty.
- A doctor's name or office contains long text or accented characters: the table and
  profile remain readable without truncating required information.
- A doctor has no photo: a neutral placeholder is shown in the profile and directory
  instead of a broken image.
- A patient has no recorded appointment results: the "Appointment results" tab shows an
  empty state.
- An appointment is approved after the schedule was loaded: reopening or refreshing the
  schedule makes the patient link active.
- The patient profile is opened for an appointment that is later cancelled: the profile is
  no longer reachable from the schedule.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Doctor creation**

- **FR-001**: The "Doctors" page MUST provide a "Create doctor" action that opens a
  creation form without leaving the page.
- **FR-002**: The creation form MUST contain: photo (optional file), first name (required),
  last name (required), middle name (optional), date of birth (required), email (required),
  specialization (required), office (required), career start year (required) and status
  (required, default "At work").
- **FR-003**: The creation form MUST provide "Confirm" and "Cancel" actions.
- **FR-004**: "Confirm" MUST remain disabled while any required field is empty, invalid, or
  while a save is in progress.
- **FR-005**: On confirmation with valid data, the system MUST store the doctor profile,
  provision a doctor account with a system-generated password, and send the credentials to
  the doctor's email address. The Receptionist MUST NOT see or choose the password.
- **FR-006**: Date of birth and career start year MUST NOT be later than the current date.
- **FR-007**: Clicking "Cancel" with any entered data MUST display "Do you really want to
  cancel? Entered data will not be saved." with "Yes" and "No". "Yes" MUST discard the
  form and return to the "Doctors" page; "No" MUST keep the form and its data.
- **FR-008**: The system MUST reject an email that already belongs to an existing user with
  "User with this email already exists" and MUST NOT create a duplicate account.
- **FR-009**: On successful creation the form MUST close and the directory MUST show the new
  doctor without requiring a manual page reload.

**Validation (create and edit)**

- **FR-010**: Validation feedback MUST appear when a field loses focus or when a save is
  attempted, and invalid fields MUST receive a red border.
- **FR-011**: Empty required fields MUST show: "Please, enter the first name",
  "Please, enter the last name", "Please, enter the email", "Please, select the date",
  "Please, choose the specialisation", "Please, choose the office", "Please, select the
  year" or "Please, choose the status", as appropriate.
- **FR-012**: An email without a valid address format MUST show "You've entered an invalid
  email".
- **FR-013**: A specialization that does not match an existing specialization MUST show
  "Invalid specialization name".
- **FR-014**: A field containing only whitespace MUST be treated as empty.
- **FR-015**: The save action for both create and edit MUST remain unavailable while any
  validation error is present.
- **FR-016**: Server-reported validation failures MUST be shown next to the affected field
  or as a form-level error, and the entered data MUST remain available for correction.
- **FR-017**: If an optional photo is attached, the system MUST show a preview before
  submission and MUST store it with the profile; if the photo cannot be stored, the profile
  MUST NOT be created or updated with a missing photo reference.

**Doctor directory, filters and status**

- **FR-018**: The "Doctors" page MUST show a table with the columns full name,
  specialization, status, date of birth and office, and a "Create doctor" action.
- **FR-019**: The status column MUST be an inline dropdown listing "At work", "On vacation",
  "Sick Day", "Sick Leave", "Self-isolation", "Leave without pay" and "Inactive".
- **FR-020**: Selecting a status MUST persist it immediately; the control MUST show a
  saving state and MUST revert to the previous value with an error message if persistence
  fails.
- **FR-021**: The directory MUST provide a "Search by name" combobox that lists doctors and,
  when a doctor is selected, shows only that doctor. Clearing the selection MUST remove
  this restriction.
- **FR-022**: The directory MUST provide a specialization filter. When an office filter is
  active, the specialization options MUST be limited to specializations of doctors working
  in that office.
- **FR-023**: The directory MUST provide an office filter. When a specialization filter is
  active, the office options MUST be limited to offices where doctors with that
  specialization work.
- **FR-024**: Name, specialization and office filters MUST combine with AND semantics, and
  the directory MUST offer a way to clear all filters at once.
- **FR-025**: When no doctor matches the active filters, the directory MUST show an
  explanatory empty state.
- **FR-026**: Selecting a doctor's full name in the table MUST open that doctor's profile.
- **FR-027**: The directory MUST give access to every doctor in the clinic, including when
  the number of doctors exceeds a single page of results.
- **FR-028**: The directory MUST show a loading state while fetching and a retryable error
  state when the fetch fails.
- **FR-029**: Only a Receptionist MUST be able to open the doctor directory and change
  doctor status.

**Profile editing**

- **FR-030**: The doctor profile page MUST open in view mode and provide an "Edit" action
  to the Doctor viewing their own profile and to a Receptionist viewing a doctor profile.
- **FR-031**: Edit mode MUST expose the doctor's photo, first name, last name, middle name,
  date of birth, specialization, office, career start year and status, plus "Save changes"
  and "Cancel" actions.
- **FR-032**: Email MUST be displayed but MUST NOT be editable after the profile is created.
- **FR-033**: "Save changes" MUST remain disabled while any required field is empty or
  invalid, or while a save is in progress.
- **FR-034**: Saving valid changes MUST persist them and return to view mode with the
  updated values displayed.
- **FR-035**: Clicking "Cancel" with unsaved changes MUST display "Do you really want to
  cancel? Changes will not be saved." with "Yes" and "No". "Yes" MUST return to view mode
  without saving; "No" MUST keep edit mode with the entered data.
- **FR-036**: Edit mode MUST enforce the same validation messages and date limits as
  creation.
- **FR-037**: A Doctor MUST be able to edit only their own profile; a Receptionist MUST be
  able to edit any doctor profile from the directory. Other roles MUST be denied.
- **FR-038**: If a profile update fails, the user MUST remain in edit mode with the entered
  data and see an error.

**Patient profile for doctors**

- **FR-039**: The Doctor's schedule MUST display each appointment's patient name.
- **FR-040**: The patient name MUST be an active link only when the appointment status is
  "Approved"; for any other status the name MUST be non-interactive and marked as
  unavailable.
- **FR-041**: Following an approved appointment's patient link MUST open the patient
  profile with two tabs: "Personal information" (default) and "Appointment results".
- **FR-042**: "Personal information" MUST show the patient's photo, first name, last name,
  middle name when present, phone number and date of birth.
- **FR-043**: "Appointment results" MUST show the patient's appointments with recorded
  results, most recent first, including the appointment date, doctor, service, complaints,
  conclusion and recommendations. It MUST show an empty state when no results exist.
- **FR-044**: The patient profile MUST be read-only for doctors in this feature.
- **FR-045**: A patient profile that cannot be loaded MUST show an explanatory error state
  with a retry action.
- **FR-046**: A user who is not entitled to a patient profile (a non-Doctor role or a
  Doctor without an approved appointment for that patient) MUST NOT see the profile
  content, including when the profile address is opened directly.
- **FR-047**: Access to doctor data MUST follow the role rules: a Receptionist can open the
  directory and any doctor profile, a Doctor can open only their own profile, and patients
  and any other role MUST be denied access to both the directory and doctor profiles.

### Key Entities

- **Doctor profile**: The clinic's record of a doctor: photo, first name, last name,
  middle name, date of birth, email, specialization, office, career start year and current
  status. A doctor profile is linked to exactly one account.
- **Doctor account**: The sign-in identity of the doctor, created together with the profile
  and provisioned with a system-generated password that is emailed to the doctor.
- **Specialization**: A medical discipline associated with doctors, services and offices.
  Used to validate and filter doctors.
- **Office**: A physical clinic location where doctors work. Used to validate and filter
  doctors.
- **Patient profile**: The patient's personal record: photo, first name, last name, middle
  name, phone number and date of birth.
- **Appointment**: A booked visit linking a patient, a doctor, a service and a time, with an
  approval status. The approval status controls whether the doctor can open the patient
  profile from the schedule.
- **Appointment result**: The medical outcome recorded for an appointment: complaints,
  conclusion and recommendations. Results are shown in the patient profile's "Appointment
  results" tab.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A Receptionist can create a doctor profile with all required data in under
  3 minutes from opening the "Create doctor" form.
- **SC-002**: 100% of creation attempts with empty or invalid required fields are blocked;
  no incomplete doctor profile is stored.
- **SC-003**: 0 duplicate doctor accounts are produced by repeated or double submissions of
  the same creation form.
- **SC-004**: A status change made in the directory is visible in the row immediately and
  remains after a page reload in 100% of successful saves.
- **SC-005**: With 500 doctors in the directory, applying any filter combination returns
  the matching rows within 1 second.
- **SC-006**: A Receptionist can locate a specific doctor using name, specialization and
  office filters in under 30 seconds.
- **SC-007**: 100% of cancellations follow the dialog contract: "Yes" never saves and "No"
  never discards entered data.
- **SC-008**: A Doctor can open an approved patient's profile in no more than 2 actions from
  the schedule page.
- **SC-009**: 0 patient profiles are reachable from not-approved appointments through the
  interface.
- **SC-010**: 95% of first-time users complete doctor creation without assistance or
  support requests.
- **SC-011**: A valid profile edit is reflected in view mode immediately and remains after a
  reload in 100% of successful saves.

## Assumptions

- The "My schedule" page is a precondition delivered by the doctor's schedule capability;
  this feature adds the patient-link behavior and the patient profile. If that page is not
  yet available, a minimal doctor schedule list is required to make User Story 2
  independently testable.
- "Appointment results" means the patient's appointment history with recorded results,
  shown read-only and most recent first. Editing appointment results is out of scope.
- A doctor's email is the account identity and is read-only after creation; changing it is
  out of scope.
- The doctor directory lists doctors regardless of status; status is changed through its own
  column and is not used as a list filter in this feature.
- The status list and default status "At work" are the ones already used by the clinic:
  "At work", "On vacation", "Sick Day", "Sick Leave", "Self-isolation", "Leave without
  pay", "Inactive".
- The directory's default order is alphabetical by full name; explicit sorting controls are
  out of scope.
- Photos are optional images with a preview; no file-size limit or additional image
  processing is required by this feature.
- Email uniqueness applies across all clinic users, not only doctors.
- The clinic operates as a single tenant; cross-clinic isolation is out of scope.
- Deactivating, archiving or deleting doctors is out of scope; only the status values above
  are changed.
- The system generates and emails passwords; password policy, delivery retries and
  credential resend flows are owned by the existing account provisioning capability.
- A Doctor can see only their own schedule and only patients from approved appointments;
  Receptionists do not use the patient profile screen in this feature.
- Date values use the user's local calendar for display, while comparisons for "not in the
  future" are date-only and ignore the time of day.
